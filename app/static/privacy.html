Privacy & data use

Privacy Policy

Analytic Sages Polaris Bot ("Polaris") is an internal team productivity and accountability tool operated by Analytic Sages. This policy describes how information is accessed and used when Polaris features are enabled.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Polaris helps authorized team members monitor key performance indicators, coordinate tasks, and track organizational progress. The dashboard is intended for authorized internal users.

2. Information We Access

Depending on the features enabled, Polaris may access information from Google Sheets and other connected services, including operational metrics, team tasks, reminders, and progress indicators.

Polaris uses Google OAuth to authenticate and access only the Google resources required for its configured functionality and the permissions granted by the authorizing user.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed by Polaris is used to:

  • Track organizational performance and progress.
  • Identify tasks requiring reminders or escalation.
  • Generate operational summaries and reports.
  • Support authorized team coordination and accountability.

4. Google User Data

Polaris uses Google APIs to provide its configured features. Google user data is not sold to third parties or used for advertising. Access is limited to the functionality described in this policy and to the permissions granted by the authorizing user.

Human access to Google user data is limited to cases where it is necessary to operate or support the service, comply with applicable law, protect against abuse, or where the user has provided appropriate consent.

Polaris' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect credentials and operational information. OAuth credentials and tokens are intended to be stored in secure environment-variable or secrets-management facilities and must not be committed to source control.

Information is retained only as necessary for operational purposes or applicable legal requirements.

6. Third-Party Services

Polaris may rely on Google APIs, hosting providers, messaging platforms, and analytics services to deliver its features. Those services are subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Access and Revocation

Users may revoke Polaris' Google account access through their Google Account security settings. Requests concerning information held by Polaris can be directed to the Analytic Sages team through the support contact published on the application's homepage.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Polaris evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page, with the revision date updated accordingly.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Analytic Sages team through its official support contact published on the application's homepage.
